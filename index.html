How it works (one time setup)
  1. Drag this button to your bookmarks bar: ⚡ Swipe Collector (show the bar with Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+B)
  2. Set Facebook's language to English (the collector reads the English labels).
  3. Click a keyword below. It opens the Ad Library search for that country.
  4. On that page click ⚡ Swipe Collector, wait ~10 s, then press Send to platform. The ads land here and get scored.
Paste data manually (if "Send to platform" is blocked)

2 · Winners sheet

Score = days running + 10 × duplicates (same copy under several IDs, or "N ads use this creative"). Long-running ads are a strong hint, not proof of sales. Untick ads that aren't direct-response selling.
